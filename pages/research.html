Research

Life cycles are central to evolution because they determine how biological individuals are formed and reproduced. Our research investigates how life cycles evolve across different levels of biological organization, from individual organisms to multispecies communities.

Theme 1: Evolution of Life Cycles

How do life cycles evolve?

Research projects

  • Predation as a driver of multicellular fragmentation modes
  • Evolution of sexual reproduction in primitive multicellularity

Theme 2: Evolutionary Transitions and Biological Individuality

How do life cycles create new biological individuals?

Research projects

  • Towards a unified definition of predation and parasites
  • Organismality defined by reproduction mode

Theme 3: Multispecies Life Cycles

How do life cycles extend across species boundaries?

Research projects

  • Multispecies multicellular fragmentations modes resulting from evolved cell death
  • Evolutionary trajectories leading to transformation of species interactions
